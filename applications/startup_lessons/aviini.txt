Two big lessons from Aviini.

Lesson 1: Never do equal equity splits based on friendship. I ran everything: operations, sourcing, listings, marketing. My co-founders were in 9-5 jobs and couldn't match the time or energy. I never renegotiated the split because one was my best friend. I should have tied equity to contribution from day one, or at least had the uncomfortable conversation early. Instead I just absorbed the resentment and burned out.

Lesson 2: Do your IP due diligence before scaling. We were unknowingly selling products with IP issues and had to shut down stores. £25K profit at 25-35% margins proved the model worked, but we built on a foundation we hadn't properly checked. Cheap lesson compared to what it could have been.

The pattern I see across both Aviini and LINQ: I keep building with people who can't or won't match my commitment level, and I avoid the hard conversations about it until it's too late.
