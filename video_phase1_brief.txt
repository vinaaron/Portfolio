FIRST YOUTUBE VIDEO
-------------------
Title: Week 0
Length: 7:35
Uploaded: 11 June 2023
Stats: 43 views, 1 like, 2 comments
Topic: Internet diary. Aaron and his best mate Avi about to
pursue dropshipping and begin the entrepreneurial journey.


WHY AARON UPLOADED IT (his words)
---------------------------------
"I didn't really care for attention. Maybe I was hoping for
views or clarity for my journey or validation that my journey
was cool and adventurous and would be what a true man should
do. This is me stretching it — it is too far back for me to
remember exactly. There would have been some external reward
I was looking for even if I wouldn't say it aloud. However,
I do honestly believe that wanting to inspire was always
there so having and accumulating some form of viewers along
the way who were really growing alongside me would have been
my ultimate desire even back then for the channel at least."


WHY DROPSHIPPING (his words)
----------------------------
"My goals were a lot more monetary at that point in time
because I thought entrepreneurship was my only way out of
the rat race. I was definitely influenced by social media
figures of the time like Andrew Tate."

Capital: £1,500 each invested into a course
Course topic: Facebook dropshipping
What they actually did: Etsy and eBay dropshipping
Co-owner: Avinash Ashok (best mate, lived together 3 years at uni)

"We ended up not using the course at all funnily enough
because the course was on FB dropshipping but the ecommerce
we dabbled in, in the end, was Etsy and eBay dropshipping."

Aaron's logic at the time:
"I have no clue what business or entrepreneurship even is or
how to do it so let's pick the most entry-level thing."

What Aviini taught him:
- Customer service
- The lack of clarity of a founder
- Time commitment
- Discipline required
- "Much more"

WHAT HAPPENED BETWEEN 2023 AND NOW (his words)
----------------------------------------------
"So many different scenarios — the splitting up Aviini, moving
away from LINQ, not knowing what to do for months, trying 3/4
apps from web apps to mobile apps."


AARON'S REFLECTION - 2023 ME vs NOW ME (his words)
--------------------------------------------------
"If 2023 me sat down with the current version of me now, damn
what a question. I would be so intrigued to think how he thinks
and think about how different I think now.

On a level of passion and energy we still have it. There have
been plenty of hiccups and lessons which that 2023 me has no
clue about. My stress tolerance is much higher, my ability to
do parallel tasks is leagues above my previous me.

Somehow that naive optimism is still there so I hope the 2023
me would be proud. One thing he may say is 'you're not jacked
yet' haha. Physicality is the endeavour I want to improve upon
in tandem and this is my weakest link right now."


CURRENT STATE OF MIND (his words)
---------------------------------
"We are still not doing anything glamorous but I would rather
have it hard like this whilst no one knows. My character is
much more developed from that younger brash, prone-to-emotions
younger version of myself.

Now I finally understand and have clarity about what I want
to do whilst having income which allows me to be fully free
to chase impact in all the other things I want to do."
