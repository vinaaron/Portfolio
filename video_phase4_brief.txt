HOW COGRAM CAME ABOUT (his words)
---------------------------------
Context around leaving LINQ:
"One of the biggest things here was there were some other
things when leaving LINQ. I left from disputes with alignment
and some core values as well — honesty policy, etc. So I am
really proud of myself for leaving for the right reasons.
There was potential of a Simon Squibb merger etc, things like
that, but once again the monetary outcome didn't matter to
me — it was the values that mattered."

What Aaron did after:
"After LINQ I was in a state where [I needed to] just return
to the inputs. I cooked up a plan to go gym, start applying
via different strategies — cold emails, different obscurer
websites. I got into contact with YC founders, got on call
with one, sent a lot of applications on LinkedIn too."

Also credits his old university teaching assistant (wants to
thank him separately — "I need to get into contact with him
and thank him for the help as well").

How Cogram actually happened:
"Maybe Cogram was more lucky. One of the co-founders reached
out to me on LinkedIn and in 1 week went through the whole
hiring process. I was in the process of others too speaking
to my old teaching assistant and everything."

On why it worked:
"Once again though it was a pleasant surprise that my
experience was really valued because a lot of coding
capability and output required the aid of an LLM tool.
However, now that sort of experience is the most valuable.
All the accomplishment and experiences I gained showcase a
very different character. It showed that hard work really
does pay off.

Now that society has valued it that way, it's time to prove
it my own way through ScrollBuddy or the personal brand."
