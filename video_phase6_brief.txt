THE CHOSEN TAKEAWAY (from Aaron's Phase 3 material)
---------------------------------------------------
Aaron picked Option D — "The north star always comes back."

Source material (his words, from Phase 3):
"There are always plenty of low moments. Times where I felt
lost, times where I felt like I was not good enough.

However, in terms of low moments, these always come. We
humans are cyclical beings, but the ultimate reality is that
if you have a north star which always guides you — the north
star always comes back and you just need to keep treasuring
that north star.

I think the greats are just able to bounce back from the
negative side of themselves much faster than the common man.
That is simply the difference between a great man and a
common man."

This is the single idea Aaron wants the viewer to walk away
with.


WHAT AARON IS *NOT* PROMISING (important — his words)
-----------------------------------------------------
Aaron explicitly pushed back on the standard "hype the next
level" framing for the CTA. He does not want the video to
promise bigger, better, more exciting content ahead.

His words:
"This is not what I'm here for. I'm here to provide the raw
and honest reality of reality. It's slow, boring and tedious
with blips of fun and delight. Enjoyment doesn't happen all
the time — sometimes there is filler.

Welcome to my life. And if you listen well, I hope there
will be nuggets that will lead you to take action in your
own life towards your own north star."

(Note for editor: the "welcome to my life" line is a natural
outro / subscribe prompt. The subscribe framing should match
this — not "subscribe for the next level," but "subscribe if
you want the real version.")


THE COMMENT CTA (his words)
---------------------------
"Describe your own pressures and reword it in how they can
make you greater."

Two-part prompt — viewers share (a) what pressure they're
under, and (b) how that pressure could be reframed as fuel.
